📜 How to Play

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to guide the serpent. On mobile, Swipe in any direction.

Consume the enchanted orbs to grow longer. Each orb grants power and score. Avoid the ancient walls and thine own tail.

⚡

Every 5 orbs consumed quickens the serpent. The hunt grows ever more intense.

Ascension

Rise from Hatchling → Serpent → Viper → Cobra → Leviathan → Ouroboros as thy power grows.

Legacy

High scores and lifetime feats are etched in stone. Chase the Hall of Legends.

🗿 About

OUROBOROS — the ancient symbol of the serpent devouring its own tail, reborn as a sacred arcade ritual.

Guide the eternal serpent through the stone labyrinth, consuming enchanted orbs to grow in power. How long can you survive the hunt?

Built with pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, Web Audio API, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no libraries — just raw craft, like the ancients intended.
