📜 How to Play
🎮

Controls

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to guide the serpent. On mobile, Swipe in any direction.

🎯

The Hunt

Consume the enchanted orbs that glow in the deep. Each orb grants power and score. Avoid the ancient walls and thine own tail.

⚡

The Challenge

Every 5 orbs consumed quickens the serpent. The abyss grows restless.

🏆

Ascension

Rise from Hatchling → Serpent → Viper → Cobra → Leviathan → Ouroboros as thy power grows.

💾

Legacy

High scores and lifetime feats are etched in the deep. Chase the Hall of Legends.

🗿 About

OUROBOROS — the ancient symbol of the serpent devouring its own tail, reborn as a sacred arcade ritual in the luminous deep.

Guide the eternal serpent through the abyssal labyrinth, consuming enchanted orbs to grow in power. How long can you survive the hunt?

Built with pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, Web Audio API, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no libraries — raw craft forged in the deep.
