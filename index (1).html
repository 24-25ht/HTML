About Quran Explorer

Quran Explorer is a complete Quran reading experience with recitation, tafsir, word-by-word analysis, search, bookmarks, account/profile with reading goals, prayer tracking, continue to ayah feature, and responsive mobile-friendly controls. Auto-scroll is available on mobile devices.
