📖 How to Play

🎮 Controls

Click or tap a piece to select it. Legal moves appear as dots. Click a highlighted square to move there.

♚ Single Player

Play as White against the AI. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.

⚔️ Local 2 Player

Two players share the same device, taking turns.

🌐 Online Multiplayer

Create a room and share the 6-character code with a friend, or enter their code to join their game.

✨ Special Moves

Castling: Click the king, then the castling square.
En Passant: Automatically shown when legal.
Promotion: Choose a piece when your pawn reaches the end.
