📜 How to Play

🎮 Controls

Click a piece to select it. Legal moves appear as dots. Click a highlighted square to move.

⚔ Single Player

Play as White against the AI (Black). Choose Apprentice for a lighter challenge or Master for a deeper-thinking opponent.

🛡 Dual Player

Two humans share the screen. White moves first. Take turns clicking your pieces.

♜ Special Moves

Castling: Click king, then the castling square. En Passant: Shown as a legal move automatically. Promotion: A selection popup appears when your pawn reaches the last rank.

⏱ Clock

Optional chess clock. When a player's time reaches zero, they lose on time.

💡 Tip

The king glows red when in check. Only legal moves are shown — you can never make an illegal move! To individually delete data, right-click Leaderboard or Dual Records. Clear Fully clears all data: saved game, leaderboard, and dual records.

💡 Troubleshooting

  • Ensure both players use the GitHub Pages HTTPS link.
  • Both players should be on the same network/Wi-Fi for best performance.
  • VPN/Adblock: These can block the connection. Try disabling them if it hangs.
  • Firewall: Ensure browsers aren't blocked from communicating with each other.
