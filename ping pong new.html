📜 How to Play
🎮

Controls

Single Player: Move your paddle with the Mouse or W/S keys.
Dual Player: P1 uses W/S, P2 uses ↑/↓ Arrow keys.

❤️

Heart System

Each player starts with 3 Hearts. When the ball passes your paddle, you lose a heart. Lose all hearts and the match is over!

🛡

Single Player: Classic

Choose Single Player → Begin Battle. Pick your difficulty and defeat the Golem by taking all 3 Hearts.

∞

Endless Mode

Choose ∞ Endless from the name screen in Single Player. You start with 5 Hearts and the Golem has infinite hearts. Every 5 points you score grants +1 Heart. Survive as long as you can and chase a high score!

⚔

Dual Player

Select Dual Player on the main menu. Player 1 uses W/S and Player 2 uses ↑/↓. First to drain the other player’s hearts wins.

🎯

Scoring & Speed

Score points each time the ball passes your opponent. The ball speeds up over time, making rallies increasingly intense!

💡

Pro Tip: Paddle Spin

Move your paddle while hitting the ball to add spin! The ball's angle changes based on your paddle's momentum — master this to outwit your foe.

🗿 About

MONOLITH PONG is an ancient-temple-themed reimagining of the classic Pong game.

Two stone guardians face off across a sacred slab, volleying an enchanted orb in an eternal contest of reflexes and honor.

Built with pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no libraries — just raw craft, like the ancients intended.
